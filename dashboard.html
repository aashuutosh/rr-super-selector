Methodology, Mathematical Architecture & Governance

Reproducible scientific framework constructed for the Rajasthan Royals Analytics War Room.

Batting Pressure Score (BPS) Formula

BPS = 0.25 · Z(SR_press) + 0.15 · Z(Avg_press) + 0.25 · Z(ΔSR) + 0.10 · Z(ΔBndry%) + 0.15 · Z(log(Vol)) - 0.10 · Z(CV_press)

Balances raw strike rate under pressure with relative elevation over individual baseline. Logarithmic volume scaling rewards verified workloads while CV penalizes boom-or-bust inconsistency.

Bowling Pressure Score (BPS_bowl) Formula

BPS_bowl = -0.25 · Z(Econ_press) + 0.20 · Z(Wkts_press) + 0.15 · Z(ΔEcon) + 0.15 · Z(Dot%_press) - 0.15 · Z(SR_press) + 0.10 · Z(log(Vol))

Negative Z-score applied to economy (lower is superior). Rewards bowlers who tighten their economy rate (ΔEcon) and generate high dot-ball percentages under pressure.
